# Blog strategy

The blog earns the same 30-minute call as the homepage. It does not sell side projects, and it is not a second homepage.

## Pillars

**D2C revenue.** Store, ads, margin, retention. Seeded with the six Shopify articles already published on rakeshkakati.com.

**B2B demand.** Pipeline, ICP, channels, and the website as the first salesperson. This pillar is empty. Posts are rewritten from public LinkedIn themes, not pasted.

## Rules

- One reader, one problem, one CTA. The CTA is the Cal.com 30-minute call: https://cal.com/rakesh-kakati-h3yw3v/30min
- D2C posts use `utm_content=dtc`. B2B posts use `utm_content=b2b`.
- 800–1,200 words for new posts. One checklist or framework. No tool roundups. No extension announcements.
- A D2C post links to `/#dtc`. A B2B post links to `/#b2b`. Both link back to `/`.
- Cadence after launch: two posts a month, alternating pillars, until B2B has six posts. Then one post a month.
- LinkedIn gets a short excerpt that points at the post. Do not paste the full piece.
