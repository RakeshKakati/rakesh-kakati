The product page is the hinge between “interested” and “sold.” Most Shopify stores underinvest in it, then wonder why conversion is stuck. These ten mistakes show up again and again.

## The ten

1. **No clear primary action.** “Add to cart” should be the hero. One dominant button, above the fold, with copy that matches intent.
2. **Weak or missing proof.** Reviews, ratings, and a line of social proof near the button can lift conversions by **10–20%**. If you have no reviews yet, use a guarantee, a founder note, or a real press mention.
3. **Thin descriptions.** A paragraph and a bullet list is not enough for a considered buy. Answer why this, and why from you.
4. **Bad images.** Multiple angles, zoom, and video where it helps. One blurry hero image is a conversion killer.
5. **Unclear price and delivery.** Hidden shipping at checkout causes abandonment. Show the cost, or “free shipping above ₹X,” and a delivery estimate, on the product page.
6. **Fake urgency.** “Only 3 left” works when it is true. A countdown that never ends burns trust.
7. **Upsells before the first add.** Bundles are useful after the visitor has committed to one product, not as a wall in front of the button.
8. **Slow load.** Compress images, cut scripts, use a fast theme. The page should feel instant.
9. **No mobile layout.** Tiny buttons and sideways image scroll kill mobile conversion. Test on a real phone.
10. **Nothing above the fold.** The first screen needs the best image, a clear title, the price, and the primary button.

Fix these and the product page becomes a revenue lever. That work is part of [D2C performance](/#dtc), next to the ads that send people here.
