Raising average order value is one of the highest-return moves on a Shopify store. Same traffic, more revenue per order. Going from **₹2,000 to ₹3,500** is structure: bundles, the moment after add-to-cart, and a little pricing psychology. It is not a louder discount.

## Bundles

“Complete the look,” “best value,” and “starter kit” answer “what else do I need?” Offer the bundle at **15–25%** off the combined price so the saving is real and the margin holds. Put it on the product page and in the cart.

## After add-to-cart

The moment someone adds one item, show one to three relevant add-ons: a complement, a care kit, or a higher tier. One click to add. Many stores see **10–20%** of orders pick up an upsell when the offer is specific.

## In the cart

“Frequently bought together,” with a clear saving, can lift AOV by **₹300–500** an order. Test it after the cart lines and before the total.

## Price and shipping

**₹3,499** often converts better than ₹3,500. “From ₹1,999,” anchored to a higher option, makes the main product feel like the sensible choice. Don’t overdo it.

A free-shipping threshold is the classic lever. “Free shipping above ₹3,500,” with a progress line — “add ₹420 for free shipping” — nudges the extra item.

On the product page, mark a mid-tier or a bundle as “most popular” or “best value,” and show the maths: “₹2,000 basic” against “₹3,200 best value, saves ₹400.” Track AOV by source and by product. Double down on what already converts at a higher ticket.

This is a [D2C performance](/#dtc) problem, not a theme problem. The ads only pay if the order is large enough.
